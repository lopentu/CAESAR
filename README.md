##  CAESAR Bio

CAESAR stands for Cognitive Automated Essay Scoring and Representations (CAESAR). We investigate language assessment in the age of AI. The ultimate goal of the project is to answer this core question: what is AI-native language assessment?

## Research Topics

### Human-Rater Rubrics & LLMs
- How do LLMs represent scoring rubrics internally?
- How do rubrics change when we move from human scoring to AI scoring?

### AI-native Rubrics Design
- What grading rubrics are most effective for AI-native language assessment?
- How should their grading rubrics change?
- What are the criteria for designing them?

### AI-native Assessment Design
- What should AI-native assessments include?
- How to ensure control and verifiability in scalable AI-native language assessment?
